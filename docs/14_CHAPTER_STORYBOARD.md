# 14-CHAPTER CINEMATIC STORYBOARD

Every scene has a professional purpose. Travel is the interaction; content is the reward. The time codes below refer to **normalized scroll progress**, not seconds. A recruiter should still have a direct map and readable resume.

## 00 — The Threshold (0–4.8%)

**Shot:** near-black transition into a moonlit castle across an enormous mist-filled valley. Close enough to read the worn stone on the cliff, distant enough to grasp the scale. Mature hooded rider prepares for flight.

**Environment:** distant towers, mountain ridges, warm pinprick windows, water, fading clouds, wet rock and sparse firelight.

**Content:** name / actual role / credible one-line mission. Start, world map, readable resume.

**Motion & exit:** minimal camera push, cloth flutter, low wind. Takeoff begins with scroll.

## 01 — Whispering Forest (4.8–12.5%)

**Shot:** rear chase camera skimming over a tree canopy, small ground lanterns visible through drifting mist. Different tree heights form a convincing silhouette.

**Environment:** conifers, roots, weathered trail, branches crossing the foreground, night insects.

**Motion:** a gentle dip and climb. The cloak responds to the speed increase. A restrained UI prompt teaches scrolling.

**Content:** one strong sentence setting the tone, no résumé dump.

## 02 — Broken Viaduct (12.5–20.5%)

**Shot:** camera enters an ancient stone span through a Gothic lancet opening, then rises above its damaged parapet.

**Environment:** pointed arch masonry, visible weight-bearing piers, broken stones, wet joints, water below, moss in sheltered creases.

**Motion:** bank through the opening, stone passes close to lens for parallax. No arcade controls required.

**Content:** transition line about choosing ambitious problems.

## 03 — The Gatehouse (20.5–28.5%)

**Shot:** a massive gate framed by asymmetric sentry towers, hinges and ironwork clearly readable.

**Environment:** gate ribs, defensive slots, banners with heavy folds, firelight, weathered buttresses.

**Motion:** doors start opening before rider arrives; camera passes through the gate under shadow. The lighting warms as the next room approaches.

**Content:** welcome into the story.

## 04 — Great Hall / About (28.5–36.5%)

**Shot:** chase camera decelerates toward a long hall with tall Gothic windows, columns and low candlelight.

**Environment:** stone floor, aged timber, chandeliers, far focal arch, banners with restrained cloth motion.

**Motion:** docked stage while real HTML biography is readable. Keep camera breathing movement under 1 degree/s.

**Content:** honest professional introduction, personal motivation and your best short bio.

## 05 — Courtyard of Identity (36.5–42.5%)

**Shot:** rise into an open courtyard with a stone compass and silent statues; vast rooflines become visible again.

**Environment:** dark evergreen plants, colonnades, carving wear and ground dampness.

**Motion:** a slow orbit of the compass before banking toward the stadium.

**Content:** Clarity, Craft and Curiosity as editable philosophy prompts.

## 06 — Aerial Trials / Skills (42.5–61.5%)

**Shot:** reveal a huge oval arena with tiered stands, buttress-supported towers, long aged banners and five brass ceremonial hoops.

**Environment:** real structural stands rather than free-floating neon objects. Hoops carry engraved divisions and metal joints; floodlight should remain controlled.

**Motion:** route bends gently through five rings. Crossing each ring brings warm metallic activation, one short toast and the next skill panel. The course remains fully scroll-driven.

**Scroll beats:** Frontend 44.6%; Backend 47.8%; Interaction 51.0%; Creative technology 54.2%; Engineering craft 57.4%. Content must be truthful, with evidence and links instead of skill percentages.

**Exit:** fifth ring unlocks; camera flies over the north side of the arena and toward the forge.

## 07 — Arcane Forge / Process (61.5–68.2%)

**Shot:** arrival in a soot-dark workshop with a hot furnace at its center. Stone and iron compress the frame after the open-air arena.

**Environment:** anvils, wall-mounted brass gears, sparks near the forge mouth, rough leather straps and dark workbenches.

**Motion:** slow, weighty rotating gears; dust rises by the fire. No random spinning magical clutter.

**Content:** Understand → Build → Refine, supported later by real process examples.

## 08 — Astral Library / Knowledge (68.2–74.2%)

**Shot:** symmetrical, tall book stacks with deep recesses. A small group of old volumes floats in low light.

**Environment:** worn leather spines, brass railings, carved shelf edges, dust in the air.

**Motion:** gentle camera translation; turning pages only where visible. Quiet after the forge.

**Content:** frameworks and tools, advanced topics and learning mentality.

## 09 — Portal Nexus / Projects (74.2–82.3%)

**Shot:** a vaulted circular chamber containing a small number of architectural portals, each with its own muted hue and structural frame.

**Environment:** stone arch rings, platform steps, etched metal details, controlled shader energy.

**Motion:** portals rotate very slowly; selecting a project opens an accessible HTML case study with real links and outcomes. No forcing the user into sublevels.

**Content:** three flagship project case studies, changeable in data.

## 10 — Chrono Tower / Experience (82.3–88.8%)

**Shot:** ascent past an immense clock mechanism with chain-driven gears, surface patina and moonlight through broken openings.

**Environment:** old brass, iron drive trains, bearings, a real sense of structure and scale.

**Motion:** slow industrial rotation; climbing camera path past a historical timeline.

**Content:** companies, dates, roles and outcomes; all editable.

## 11 — Hall of Relics / Milestones (88.8–92.7%)

**Shot:** a short, intimate gallery of three sculptures on heavy plinths. Sharp beams of light separate the pieces from shadow.

**Environment:** sparse display stands, weathered metal, stone joints, controlled reflections.

**Motion:** subtle light play, no giant reward burst.

**Content:** real milestones and achievements only. Placeholder labels are visibly provisional.

## 12 — Owl Observatory / Contact (92.7–97.3%)

**Shot:** an open tower terrace; the world stretches below in dark blue distance. An owl waits by weathered brass instruments.

**Environment:** parapets, feathers, sky, telescope silhouette and moving distant clouds.

**Motion:** camera settles; feather and cloth shift gently, sound becomes still.

**Content:** email, LinkedIn, GitHub, and resume download when filled in.

## 13 — Golden Horizon / Epilogue (97.3–100%)

**Shot:** rider launches into a brighter edge of dawn; the kingdom's silhouette reappears in its entirety.

**Environment:** atmospheric color shift, distant spires, warmer sky near the horizon.

**Motion:** extended, smooth pullback. No explosion of particles. The finish belongs to the viewer and the story.

**Content:** Fly again, direct resume, final invitation to collaborate.
